# GREENHOUSE PHYSICS DATASET KNOWLEDGE BASE

> **Purpose**
>
> This file is the scientific source-of-truth for the greenhouse synthetic-dataset generator.
> Codex/agents MUST read this file before implementing or modifying the physics simulator.
>
> The project does **not** claim that every equation below is copied verbatim from one paper.
> The simulator is a **reduced-order physics-based greenhouse model** assembled from established
> energy/mass-balance relationships and simplified to match the variables that are actually
> available to this project.

---

## 1. Scientific provenance rule

Every implemented equation or submodel must be labelled internally/documented with one of:

- `DIRECT`: equation/relationship is a standard relation or is directly adopted from the cited source.
- `ADAPTED`: source establishes the physical relationship, but notation/state representation is adapted.
- `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`: the project intentionally simplifies several literature models into a lower-order equation.

**Never write that a `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM` equation is “the equation of paper X”.**

The scientific claim of this project is:

> Historical meteorological reanalysis is used as external forcing for a reduced-order,
> physics-based greenhouse simulator. Synthetic indoor trajectories are generated by
> integrating coupled energy, water-vapour and root-zone water balances.

# 3. Simulator state definition

The minimum dynamic state vector is:

\[
x_t =
\begin{bmatrix}
T_{in,t} \\
\rho_{v,in,t} \\
T_{soil,t} \\
\theta_t
\end{bmatrix}
\]

where:

- \(T_{in}\): greenhouse air temperature, °C
- \(\rho_{v,in}\): indoor water-vapour density, kg m\(^{-3}\)
- \(T_{soil}\): effective pot/root-zone soil temperature, °C
- \(\theta\): volumetric root-zone water content, m\(^3\) m\(^{-3}\)

Derived outputs:

- \(RH_{in}\): indoor relative humidity, %
- \(VPD_{in}\): indoor vapour-pressure deficit, kPa
- \(Lux_{in}\): indoor illuminance, lux

External disturbances:

\[
d_t =
[T_{out}, RH_{out}, G_{out}, v_{wind}, p_{surf}]
\]

Control inputs:

\[
u_t =
[u_{pump}, u_{fan}, u_{grow}, u_{vent}]
\]

The actuator states are **control inputs**, not weather observations.

### Research basis

López-Cruz et al. studied a first-principles greenhouse model with three core states:
air temperature, air humidity and upper soil temperature. This establishes strong precedent for
using a deliberately low-order state representation instead of modelling every greenhouse surface.

Source:
- I.L. López-Cruz et al. (2008), *Modeling of Greenhouse Climate Using Evolutionary Algorithms*
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.17660/ActaHortic.2008.801.42
- Publisher: https://ishs.org/ishs-article/801_42/

Nguyen et al. later presented an interpretable differentiable physics-based greenhouse simulator
whose next state is obtained by solving a differential-equation system and whose physical parameters
can be fitted from real greenhouse data.

Source:
- N.M. Nguyen et al. (2022), *Differentiable Physics-based Greenhouse Simulation*
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.11502
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.11502

# 4. E0 — Outdoor vapour state

## E0.1 Saturation vapour pressure

\[
e_s(T)=0.6108
\exp\left(\frac{17.27T}{T+237.3}\right)
\]

Units: kPa when \(T\) is in °C.

**Provenance:** `DIRECT`

## E0.2 Actual outdoor vapour pressure

\[
e_{out}
=
\frac{RH_{out}}{100}e_s(T_{out})
\]

**Provenance:** `DIRECT`

## E0.3 Outdoor vapour density

\[
\rho_{v,out}
=
\frac{1000e_{out}}
{R_v(T_{out}+273.15)}
\]

with:

\[
R_v = 461.5\;J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}
\]

**Provenance:** `DIRECT` thermodynamic conversion using the ideal-gas relation for water vapour.

### Scientific source

FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 defines the standard saturation/actual vapour-pressure
relationships used in evapotranspiration calculations.

- R.G. Allen, L.S. Pereira, D. Raes, M. Smith (1998)
- *Crop Evapotranspiration — Guidelines for Computing Crop Water Requirements*
- Official FAO source:
  https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm

# 5. E1 — Solar transmission through the greenhouse cover

\[
G_{in,t}
=
\tau_{sw}G_{out,t}
\]

where:

- \(G_{out}\): external shortwave radiation, W m\(^{-2}\)
- \(G_{in}\): transmitted shortwave radiation, W m\(^{-2}\)
- \(\tau_{sw}\): shortwave cover transmittance

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

This is the reduced form of the cover-transmission process. More detailed greenhouse models
separate beam, diffuse and reflected radiation and allow transmittance to vary with incidence angle.

### Research sources and what is inherited

1. **Zhang et al. (2020)**  
   *Dynamic energy balance model of a glass greenhouse: An experimental validation and solar energy analysis*  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2020.117281  
   Publisher:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544220303881

   **Inherited:** greenhouse energy balance must explicitly account for cover transmittance/absorbance
   and transmitted solar energy.

2. **Baneshi, Gonome & Maruyama (2020)**  
   *Wide-range spectral measurement of radiative properties of commercial greenhouse covering plastics...*  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2020.118535  
   Publisher:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544220316431

   **Inherited:** \(\tau_{sw}\) is a material property and should be selected from the real cover
   datasheet/measurement/literature range, not randomized hourly.

# 6. E2 — Natural and forced ventilation

Adopt the project ventilation form:

\[
\dot V_t =
\dot V_{leak}
+
C_d A_v u_{vent}
\sqrt{
C_w v_{wind,t}^{2}
+
2gH_v
\frac{|T_{in,t}-T_{out,t}|}
{T_{in,t}+273.15}
}
+
\dot V_{fan,max}u_{fan,t}
\]

where:

- \(\dot V\): air-exchange volumetric flow, m\(^3\) s\(^{-1}\)
- \(A_v\): effective vent opening area
- \(C_d\): discharge coefficient
- \(C_w\): wind-effect coefficient
- \(H_v\): effective vertical opening scale
- \(u_{vent}\): vent opening fraction/state
- \(u_{fan}\): fan state
- \(\dot V_{fan,max}\): fan airflow from datasheet/measurement

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

This equation represents the accepted physical drivers:
**wind pressure + thermal buoyancy + forced fan flow**.
Its coefficients MUST be treated as geometry-dependent/calibratable parameters.

### Research source

M. Teitel & J. Tanny (1999),
*Natural ventilation of greenhouses: experiments and model*,
Agricultural and Forest Meteorology 96, 59–70.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-1923(99)00041-6
- Publisher:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168192399000416

**Inherited:** opening geometry, wind speed and temperature difference govern greenhouse ventilation;
ventilation changes both greenhouse temperature and humidity.

# 7. E3 — Root-zone water stress

Define:

\[
\theta_{thr}
=
\theta_{fc}
-
p(\theta_{fc}-\theta_{wp})
\]

and:

\[
K_s(\theta)=
\begin{cases}
1, & \theta\geq\theta_{thr}\\
\dfrac{\theta-\theta_{wp}}
{(1-p)(\theta_{fc}-\theta_{wp})},
& \theta_{wp}<\theta<\theta_{thr}\\
0, & \theta\leq\theta_{wp}
\end{cases}
\]

where:

- \(\theta_{fc}\): field capacity
- \(\theta_{wp}\): wilting-point water content
- \(p\): depletion fraction before stress
- \(K_s\): water-stress factor in \([0,1]\)

**Provenance:** `ADAPTED`

This is a volumetric-water-content representation of the FAO-56 root-zone stress concept.

### Source

FAO-56:
https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm

**Inherited:** crop evapotranspiration/transpiration must be reduced when available root-zone water
falls below a stress threshold.

# 8. E4 — Greenhouse crop/pot evapotranspiration

First compute indoor VPD:

\[
VPD_{in,t}
=
\max\left[e_s(T_{in,t})-e_{in,t},0\right]
\]

Then use the project reduced transpiration form:

\[
\dot m_{ET,t}
=
A_{crop}
K_s(\theta_t)
\left[
k_R G_{crop,t}
+
k_D VPD_{in,t}
\right]
\]

with:

\[
G_{crop,t}
=
G_{in,t}
+
\frac{\eta_{rad,LED}P_{LED}}{A_{crop}}u_{grow,t}
\]

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

Do NOT claim that the above linear expression is copied verbatim from Boulard & Wang.
The source is used to justify the **driver structure**: crop transpiration responds strongly to
radiation and vapour-pressure conditions and can be predicted from climatic conditions.

Parameters \(k_R\) and \(k_D\) are calibration parameters.

### Research source

T. Boulard & S. Wang (2000),
*Greenhouse crop transpiration simulation from external climate conditions*.

Journal version:
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-1923(99)00082-9
- Publisher:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168192399000829

Related ISHS paper:
- https://ishs.org/ishs-article/534_27/
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.17660/ActaHortic.2000.534.27

### Auxiliary QA

Open-Meteo `et0_fao_evapotranspiration` is **reference outdoor ET₀**, not greenhouse crop ET.
It may be retained for QA/context but must not be directly renamed to greenhouse ET.

# 9. E5 — Indoor water-vapour mass balance

\[
\frac{d\rho_{v,in}}{dt}
=
\frac{\dot m_{ET}}{V_g}
+
\frac{\dot V}{V_g}
(\rho_{v,out}-\rho_{v,in})
-
\frac{\dot m_{cond}}{V_g}
\]

where:

- \(V_g\): greenhouse air volume
- \(\dot m_{ET}\): water vapour source from crop/pot evapotranspiration
- ventilation transports vapour between indoor/outdoor air
- \(\dot m_{cond}\): optional condensation sink

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM` from water-vapour mass conservation.

### Saturation closure

\[
\rho_{v,sat}(T)
=
\frac{1000e_s(T)}
{R_v(T+273.15)}
\]

After a numerical integration substep:

\[
\rho_{v,in}
\leftarrow
\min(\rho_{v,in}^{*},\rho_{v,sat}(T_{in}))
\]

Excess water vapour can be recorded as condensation.

### Research support

1. **Liu et al. (2021)**  
   *A fast and practical one-dimensional transient model for greenhouse temperature and humidity*  
   Computers and Electronics in Agriculture 186, 106186  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2021.106186  
   Publisher:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168169921002039

   **Inherited:** temperature and humidity are coupled transient greenhouse states and should be
   evolved mechanistically rather than generated independently.

2. **Teitel & Tanny (1999)**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-1923(99)00041-6

   **Inherited:** ventilation simultaneously affects indoor temperature and humidity.

# 10. E6 — Derive indoor RH from temperature and vapour state

First:

\[
e_{in,t}
=
\frac{\rho_{v,in,t}R_v(T_{in,t}+273.15)}{1000}
\]

Then:

\[
RH_{in,t}
=
100\frac{e_{in,t}}{e_s(T_{in,t})}
\]

Clamp only for numerical safety:

\[
0 \le RH_{in} \le 100
\]

**Provenance:** `DIRECT` thermodynamic/FAO humidity relationship.

### Scientific rule

`RH_inside` MUST NOT be generated as an independent random/regrression column.
It is a derived consequence of \(T_{in}\) and the indoor vapour state.

Source:
- FAO-56:
  https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm

# 11. E7 — Indoor-air energy balance

The project reduced-order energy equation is:

\[
C_{eff}
\frac{dT_{in}}{dt}
=
Q_{solar}
+
Q_{cover}
+
Q_{vent}
+
Q_{soil}
-
Q_{latent}
+
Q_{grow}
+
Q_{cond}
\]

with:

\[
Q_{solar}=\eta_aA_fG_{in}
\]

\[
Q_{cover}=UA_c(T_{out}-T_{in})
\]

\[
Q_{vent}
=
\rho_{air}c_p\dot V(T_{out}-T_{in})
\]

\[
Q_{soil}
=
h_{as}A_s(T_{soil}-T_{in})
\]

\[
Q_{latent}
=
L_v\dot m_{ET}
\]

\[
Q_{grow}
=
\eta_{heat,LED}P_{LED}u_{grow}
\]

Optional condensation heat release:

\[
Q_{cond}=L_v\dot m_{cond}
\]

Thus:

\[
\frac{dT_{in}}{dt}
=
\frac{
\eta_aA_fG_{in}
+
UA_c(T_{out}-T_{in})
+
\rho_{air}c_p\dot V(T_{out}-T_{in})
+
h_{as}A_s(T_{soil}-T_{in})
-
L_v\dot m_{ET}
+
\eta_{heat,LED}P_{LED}u_{grow}
+
L_v\dot m_{cond}
}{
C_{eff}
}
\]

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

### Research sources and inherited components

1. **Zhang et al. (2020)**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2020.117281  
   **Inherited:** dynamic energy balance; solar transfer through greenhouse cover; internal/external
   heat-transfer processes; experimental validation of a multi-state greenhouse model.

2. **Reyes-Rosas et al. (2017)**  
   *Development of a single energy balance model for prediction of temperatures inside a naturally
   ventilated greenhouse with polypropylene soil mulch*  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2017.08.020  
   Publisher:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168169917307378

   **Inherited:** coupled energy balances for greenhouse air, cover, plant/mulch/soil in a naturally
   ventilated greenhouse.

3. **Nauta et al. (2024; published online 2023)**  
   *A new greenhouse energy model for predicting the year-round interior microclimate of a
   commercial greenhouse in Ontario, Canada*  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inpa.2023.06.002  
   Publisher:
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214317323000550

   **Inherited:** use of a lumped-capacitance thermal representation to predict greenhouse interior
   microclimate from exterior conditions and operating conditions.

### Air density

If surface pressure is available:

\[
\rho_{air}
\approx
\frac{p}{R_d(T_{in}+273.15)}
\]

where \(R_d \approx 287.05\;J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}\).

Use consistent units: pressure must be converted to Pa before this equation.

# 12. E8 — Effective greenhouse soil/pot temperature

Use one effective root-zone thermal state:

\[
C_s
\frac{dT_{soil}}{dt}
=
h_{as}A_s(T_{in}-T_{soil})
+
\eta_sA_sG_{in}
-
Q_{soil,loss}
\]

Minimal v1 may represent the slow deeper-soil/pot loss as:

\[
Q_{soil,loss}=U_sA_s(T_{soil}-T_{base})
\]

or omit it only if calibration/validation shows that the simpler form is stable.

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

### Research basis

- López-Cruz et al. (2008) explicitly use an upper-layer soil-temperature state:
  https://doi.org/10.17660/ActaHortic.2008.801.42
- Zhang et al. (2020) model multiple soil thermal layers:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2020.117281
- Reyes-Rosas et al. (2017) include greenhouse soil layers in the energy-balance system:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2017.08.020

**Project simplification:** multiple physical soil layers are lumped into one effective pot/root-zone
thermal state because the project does not observe every soil layer.

# 13. E9 — Root-zone soil-moisture balance

\[
V_{root}
\frac{d\theta}{dt}
=
q_{pump}u_{pump}
-
\frac{\dot m_{ET}}{\rho_w}
-
q_{drain}
\]

Use a simple gravitational drainage closure:

\[
q_{drain}
=
V_{root}k_d
\max(\theta-\theta_{fc},0)
\]

Therefore:

\[
\frac{d\theta}{dt}
=
\frac{q_{pump}u_{pump}}{V_{root}}
-
\frac{\dot m_{ET}}{\rho_wV_{root}}
-
k_d\max(\theta-\theta_{fc},0)
\]

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM` from root-zone water conservation.

### Source

FAO-56 provides the root-zone water-balance / crop-water-stress framework:

https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm

### Greenhouse rule

For a covered greenhouse/pot system:

\[
q_{rain}=0
\]

unless the real hardware explicitly captures rainfall and routes it into the irrigation system.

External Open-Meteo soil moisture is NOT substituted into this state.

# 14. E10 — Indoor illuminance

Because the project sensor is BH1750 (lux) while meteorological radiation is W m\(^{-2}\), use
luminous efficacy.

Preferred form when direct and diffuse radiation are available:

\[
Lux_{solar,t}
=
\tau_{vis}
\left(
K_{dir,t}G_{dir,t}
+
K_{dif,t}G_{dif,t}
\right)
\]

and:

\[
Lux_{in,t}
=
Lux_{solar,t}
+
Lux_{grow,max}u_{grow,t}
\]

Fallback when only global shortwave radiation is retained:

\[
Lux_{in,t}
=
K_{lum,t}\tau_{vis}G_{out,t}
+
Lux_{grow,max}u_{grow,t}
\]

**Provenance:** `PROJECT_REDUCED_FORM`

Do not assume luminous efficacy is a universal constant. It depends on sky/atmospheric conditions.
Use literature values only as a prior and calibrate with BH1750 measurements.

### Research source

A.H. Fakra, H. Boyer, F. Miranville, D. Bigot (2011),
*A simple evaluation of global and diffuse luminous efficacy for all sky conditions in tropical
and humid climate*, Renewable Energy 36(1), 298–306.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2010.06.042
- Publisher:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960148110003150
- Open manuscript:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.5259

**Inherited:** irradiance-to-illuminance conversion through global/diffuse luminous efficacy and the
fact that efficacy varies with sky condition in tropical-humid climates.

# 16. Numerical integration rule

Open-Meteo forcing is hourly, but the greenhouse thermal/humidity dynamics should be integrated at a
shorter internal timestep.

Recommended:

```text
weather forcing interval: 1 hour
internal physics timestep: 60–300 seconds
saved dataset interval: 1 hour
```

Use interpolation/zero-order hold as explicitly documented.

Preferred numerical integration:
- RK4, or
- `scipy.integrate.solve_ivp`

Avoid using one explicit Euler step of 3600 seconds without a stability study.

Nguyen et al. provide precedent for advancing greenhouse state by solving a differential system:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.11502

# 18. Synthetic-trajectory rule

Diversity should be introduced by varying **scenario/parameter sets**, not by independently adding
large random noise to output columns.

For simulation \(i\):

\[
p^{(i)} \sim P(p)
\]

Then hold physically static parameters constant over that trajectory unless the parameter is explicitly
time-varying (e.g. actuator state, crop growth stage).

Each row should include:

```text
sim_id
parameter_set_id
```

Recommended outputs:

```text
timestamp

# external forcing
temperature_outside
humidity_outside
shortwave_radiation
direct_radiation
diffuse_radiation
wind_speed
surface_pressure

# dynamic/derived greenhouse states
temperature_inside
humidity_inside
vapor_density_inside
vpd_inside
soil_temperature_inside
soil_moisture_inside
light_lux_inside

# intermediate physical diagnostics
ventilation_rate
evapotranspiration_rate
condensation_rate

# controls
pump_state
fan_state
grow_light_state
vent_state

# provenance
sim_id
parameter_set_id
```

Intermediate physics columns should be retained even if the downstream GRU/LSTM does not consume them,
because they allow physics-consistency auditing.

# 19. Required sanity checks

The generator must fail or warn loudly when any of the following occurs unexpectedly:

```text
RH_inside < 0 or RH_inside > 100
soil_moisture_inside < wilting/specified lower physical bound
soil_moisture_inside > saturation/specified upper physical bound
negative absolute pressure
negative ventilation flow
negative evapotranspiration when no condensation process is intended
NaN / inf in state variables
solar illuminance > 0 while solar irradiance = 0 and grow light = OFF
large one-step temperature jumps inconsistent with the configured timestep
```

Also verify directionality:

- increasing ventilation should tend to pull indoor temperature/humidity toward outside conditions;
- increasing solar radiation should generally increase greenhouse heat/light input;
- pump ON should increase root-zone water storage;
- evapotranspiration should reduce root-zone water and increase indoor vapour;
- drier soil should reduce transpiration through \(K_s\).

- remove a physical coupling merely to simplify code,
- replace the physics simulator with an arbitrary regression/GAN/LLM generator,
- treat outdoor Open-Meteo soil values as greenhouse pot sensor truth,
- generate RH independently from temperature/vapour,
- fabricate literature citations,
- describe project-reduced equations as verbatim equations from cited papers,
- hard-code unknown physical parameters without recording their provenance.

If a physical parameter is unknown, expose it explicitly as configuration and mark it
`LITERATURE_PRIOR` or `TO_CALIBRATE`.
